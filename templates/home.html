Falguni Sanjay Nayar — Pioneer of India’s Omnichannel Beauty Revolution

At the age of 49, after a celebrated two-decade career leading Kotak Mahindra’s investment banking division, Falguni Nayar stepped into the uncharted waters of e-commerce. Who is Falguni Nayar?

The Investment Banker

Armed with an MBA from IIM Ahmedabad, Falguni spent 19 formative years at Kotak Mahindra Group, rising to Managing Director of Kotak Mahindra Capital Company and advising top Indian conglomerates on public listings and mergers.

Market Analysis

The Vision that Revolutionized Indian Retail

Before 2012, India’s beauty and personal care landscape was fragmented, unorganized, and fraught with counterfeit products. Most consumers purchased beauty products from local neighborhood counters with restricted shelf space and minimal product advice.

Falguni Nayar identified a structural opportunity: Indian consumers were aspirational, increasingly digitally literate, and hungry for genuine luxury and drugstore brands. Rather than operating as an uncurated open marketplace, she insisted on an inventory-led model where Nykaa purchased directly from authorized brand manufacturers, guaranteeing 100% authenticity.

Her vision united commerce with education: Nykaa introduced beauty tutorials, expert advice columns, shade-finder tools, and authentic customer reviews, empowering millions of women and men across Tier 1, 2, and 3 cities to discover beauty with confidence.
